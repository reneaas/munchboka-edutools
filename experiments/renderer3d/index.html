The same mathematics, two renderers

JSXGraph 1.13.3 and Three.js 0.180.0. Shared scene data, orthographic views, fixed-size strokes, and KaTeX labels. This experiment does not change the directive.

Drag either figure to rotate both views. Camera controls also work with a keyboard. The hidden-edge pass is implemented in Three.js only; JSXGraph uses its native depth ordering.
